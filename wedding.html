Our Story

Six years, one orchard, and a very persistent dog.

We met on a rainy Tuesday over a shared umbrella and a mutual dislike of the coffee at the place on 5th Street. Neither of us left with the umbrella we came with, and somehow that felt like the beginning of something.

Now, after six years, three apartments, and one very opinionated rescue dog named Biscuit, we're ready to say it in front of everyone who matters most to us. We can't wait to celebrate with you.
